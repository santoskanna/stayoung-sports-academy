About Us

Where Champions Are Made

STAYOUNG Sports Academy & Unisex Gym is Ranipet's premier multi-sport training facility. We provide quality coaching and infrastructure for aspiring athletes of all ages — from young beginners to competitive adults.

Our facility houses badminton courts, cricket turfs, a dedicated skating ground, archery and silambam training, and a fully-equipped air-conditioned unisex gym. Whether you're here to compete or just stay fit, STAYOUNG is your home.
